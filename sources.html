Resolution and sampling

The review master is 2400 × 1350 / 60fps. The approved F1 source is native 2400 × 1350. New budget/development and screen-entry WebGL were rendered at 1920 × 1080 and composited to the master; DOM text was rasterized at the composite dimensions. A 60fps file is not a guarantee of live performance on every device. Lightweight website copies are 1280 × 720 / 30fps. All files are silent.

Interactive Lab

The six groups are two-car launch; a car driving out of a budget bar; wing iteration; screen entry/exit; a dot plot writing Mean / Median; and connected character motion. The budget-car example replaces the previous outline/photo lesson. The dot plot replaces the previous slowed-down typography default. Startup requests no 3D models and waits for Start; models load when selected. Prepared F1 models are shared across lessons. The full-team inspection remains in the local version. Slow play, scrubbing, phase jumps, frame stepping and text controls sample original functions.
