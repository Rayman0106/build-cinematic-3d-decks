THE METHOD / WHAT THE SKILL HELPS BUILD

An idea survives
the transition.

Give your AI a report, a topic, or an existing project.
The skill guides the design and implementation in your workspace.

  1. 01

    Find the through-line

    Turn a question, evidence and conclusion into a storyboard. Choose an object that can carry the argument between pages.

    Story → shot plan
  2. 02

    Make the handoff meaningful

    Register a model with its outline. Follow actual chart points into a title. Let a camera move explain where the next page comes from.

    3D ↔ outline ↔ 2D
  3. 03

    Deliver something you can keep

    Check assets and motion, prepare before Start, and ship readable slides, an offline HTML deck, source and a reproducible build.

    Scene → usable deck
